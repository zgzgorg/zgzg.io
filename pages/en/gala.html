About the ZaiGeZaiGu Gala

Since 2015, ZaiGeZaiGu (载歌在谷) has held the Lunar New Year Gala 11 years in a row. The Gala has wide attention from many communities, especially the Chinese community in North America. Our media partners include Chihuo Xiaofendui, Weibo, Sing Tao Radio, Singtao TV, Chinese American Voice, Ximalaya and more.
The Gala has also appeared on CCTV-4 several times, sending Lunar New Year greetings to Chinese people around the world on behalf of the overseas Chinese community.
