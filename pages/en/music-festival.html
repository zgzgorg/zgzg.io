Music Festival · Call for Bands

Defying Gravity: Let Music Float Above the Crowd

The world keeps running on inertia: busy, repetitive, going with the flow.
But music never follows the crowd.
To go against the current is to grow your own way; to float is to break free from the ground.

The theme of the first ZaiGeZaiGu Music Festival is Defying Gravity, and the call for bands is open.

We want to break the one-way model where the band plays and the crowd only watches. We want musicians to meet their listeners up close, so that music and audience move each other.

Any style, any experience level. If your music has feeling, something to say and power, this is your stage.

01 All Styles Welcome: 6-8 Bands Wanted

Any genre is welcome:

Rock / Folk / Pop / Chinese-style / Punk / Jazz / Electronic / Indie / Alternative / Original

02 Key Requirement: Build the Buzz and Create Together

To bring the Defying Gravity theme to life and give the stage warmth and stories, the bands help with promotion before the show. The stage is not only about show day; it is about growing together the whole way. Bands help with early promotion and on-site planning:

  • ✅ Rehearsal footage: clips from rehearsals, everyday band life, songwriting moments and jam sessions
  • ✅ Content sharing: let the organizers use and share your content, and share our posts, so we reach more people together
  • ✅ Interactive moments: plan time for audience interaction, music sharing and conversation with listeners

We want the show to be the result of preparing and growing together, not a last-minute appearance. Listeners get to know you early and look forward to your set, so the show feels like a meeting both sides have waited for.

03 What You Get
  • 🎵 Close contact with listeners: break the wall between stage and crowd, and build a real, loyal live audience
  • 🎵 Full photo coverage: high-resolution photos of rehearsals and the show for your portfolio and social media
  • 🎵 Musician community: share the stage with bands of other styles, swap ideas and find new inspiration

The world moves forward; we go against the current. Everything sinks; we float.

The most precious part of music is never the stage lights. It is the people who listen, who feel it with you, and who stop for your passion.

Break the inertia, break free from the ordinary, and let your voice float above the Bay Area.

On the stage of the first ZaiGeZaiGu Music Festival, Defying Gravity, we look forward to sharing the stage with you and meeting what we love.
