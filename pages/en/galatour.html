2025 Fall Garden Party

Date: Sunday, November 2, 2025, 12 PM - 5 PM Pacific Time

Venue: West Valley College, Parking Lot 3, 16000 Melsa Ln, Saratoga, CA 95070

What Is the Garden Party?

The festival is here! Far from home, do you miss the lanterns and the joy of the holidays? Want to join the fun but do not know where to go?

Folk activities, traditional games and fun interactive booths are all at the ZaiGeZaiGu (载歌在谷) Garden Party! It is the biggest and liveliest Garden Party in the Bay Area, and the one most full of Chinese tradition. It is great for the whole family, so bring your children to experience Chinese culture! Many booths are free, and many give gifts and prizes!
